The bundled compressed JSON is a reduced extract of the official German BLS 4.0 XLSX:
BLS code, German food name, energy (kcal), protein, fat, available
carbohydrate, fiber, sugar and sodium per 100 g. Original values are not
modified. Other fields and provenance columns are omitted to reduce size.
